## Brand & Style

This design system translates the rigorous functionalism and geometric grammar of the Bauhaus movement into an AI-powered intelligence platform for Korean fine art and design university entrance exams. The UI rejects the homogenized neo-brutalist or blurred glass aesthetic of contemporary SaaS in favor of an authoritative, print-grade editorial architecture. It directly channels the foundational theories of Wassily Kandinsky (*Point and Line to Plane*) alongside Herbert Bayer’s typographic discipline, adapted precisely for high-stakes admission analysis across 17 tier-one Korean art institutions (such as SNU, Hongik, K-Arts, and Kookmin).

The tone is scholarly, decisive, and structural. The experience addresses students, parents, and studio directors who require transparent, evidence-backed evaluation rather than opaque algorithmic scoring. Visual hierarchy is established through disciplined grid frameworks, stark planar surfaces, high-contrast text settings, and functional primary color accents that serve strictly communicative and pedagogical purposes.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in early modernism, calibrated according to purposeful ratios:

- **Warm Off-White (`#F4F1E8`) — 65%**: The foundational canvas plane. Evokes raw, unbleached lithography paper and heavy watercolor board, setting a grounded, tactile atmosphere without digital glare.
- **Charcoal Black (`#151515`) — 20%**: Architectural ink. Used for structural grid rules, structural frames, typography, and heavy geometric icons.
- **Surface White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure geometric relief. Applied strictly to analytical card planes, data inspection viewports, and active input fields to establish sharp figure-ground separation.
- **Secondary Gray (`#767676`)**: De-emphasized editorial metadata, prospectus citation footnotes, and non-active state borders.
- **Cobalt Blue (`#1647D8`) — 8% (Primary Accent)**: Dedicated exclusively to validated intelligence—retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) verified admissions guidelines, confirmed tier matches, and active analytical states.
- **Vermilion Red (`#E34234`) — 4% (Secondary Accent)**: Used for disqualification risks, practical exam time constraints, non-negotiable CSAT cutoff alerts, and critical anomalies.
- **Mustard Yellow (`#EABF36`) — 3% (Tertiary Accent)**: Reserved for document evaluation criteria highlights, cautionary advisory flags, and comparative candidate benchmarking.

Gradients, dropshadow blurs, and spectral neon fills are explicitly prohibited. Color operates as categorical data encoding.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances technical numeric indexing with Korean editorial rigor. Primary headlines, indexing figures, quota tallies, and admissions matrices employ `Space Grotesk` to honor the geometric character of European modernism. All body copy, official evaluation excerpts, and administrative terms utilize `Noto Sans` (with direct fallback to Pretendard and SUIT), ensuring uncompromised legibility across Korean art terminology (기초디자인, 발상과표현, 조형실기, 학생부종합).

- Use all-caps with generous tracking (`0.05em` to `0.08em`) for metric markers, university codes, and admission phase tags (`label-lg`, `label-md`, `label-sm`).
- Paragraph leading in Korean body text remains open (`1.55` to `1.6`) to accommodate complex ideograms and mixed numeric dates without visual crowding.
- Bold weights (`700`) must be deployed deliberately to anchor grid coordinates, while fine weights remain reserved for supporting institutional evidence.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout relies on an asymmetrical, 12-column Swiss/Bauhaus architectural grid. Content is never scattered organically; every block aligns flush against hard layout axis lines.

- **Desktop (1280px and above)**: 12 columns with 24px gutters and 48px outer margins. The workspace accommodates a fixed 320px geometric sidebar for candidate profile parameters, an 8-column center plane for multi-university admission scenarios, and a 4-column right-hand split for live RAG prospectus quotations.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8 columns with 20px gutters and 32px margins. Secondary analysis panels fold into segmented tabbed planes.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with 16px gutters and 20px margins. Layout stacks vertically with horizontal planar scrolling reserved for multi-school cross-evaluation tables.
- **Horizontal & Vertical Rhythm**: The base grid unit is 8px. Dividers are not merely visual separations but continuous structural lines running across the canvas, directly reflecting Bauhaus architectural drafting plates.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial depth is achieved strictly through **planar stacking and planar contrast**, rejecting all blurred drop shadows and skeuomorphic lighting.

- **Surface Tiers**: The lowest elevation layer is the Warm Off-White canvas (`#F4F1E8`). Analysis cards, document inspectors, and input groups sit on top as Surface White (`#FFFFFF`) planes.
- **Linework as Elevation**: Boundary definitions rely on crisp 1px solid borders in Charcoal Black (`#151515`) or muted Secondary Gray (`#767676`).
- **Hard Projection (Active Focus)**: When an active card or university inspection modal requires elevation, it utilizes a 0-blur, high-contrast projection: `3px 3px 0px #151515`. This creates tactile, print-register dimensional depth without modern ambient softness.
- **Overlays**: System modals introduce an absolute solid `#151515` overlay set at 80% opacity, cutting the background into high-contrast relief.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary directly synthesizes Kandinsky's seminal *Point and Line to Plane* doctrine into UI semantics:

- **Point / Circle**: Represents the **Individual Candidate**. Circular avatars, circular practical exam performance nodes, and circular percentile points denote human performance input.
- **Square / Rectangle**: Represents the **Institutional Frame / Empirical Data**. University admission quotas, CSAT cut-off matrix grids, and RAG document containers must take strict rectangular forms.
- **Triangle**: Represents **Decision / Advisory Caution**. Triangular badges mark practical exam time thresholds, cut-off warnings, and severe quota variance alerts.
- **Line**: Represents **Direct RAG Evidence & Synthesis**. Hairline vectors, directional brackets, and connective rules bind candidate specs directly to verbatim official prospectus clauses.
- **Radii**: Uniformly maintained at 6px to 8px for containers, tags, and interactive inputs. Sharp, disciplined corner geometry ensures interfaces convey institutional gravity while retaining contemporary digital polish.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Admissions Intelligence Run)**: Solid Charcoal Black (`#151515`) background, Surface White (`#FFFFFF`) bold text, 1px solid `#151515` border, 8px corner radius. Hover shifts the background instantly to Cobalt Blue (`#1647D8`) with zero ease-in softness.
- **Secondary Action (Prospectus Comparison)**: Surface White (`#FFFFFF`) background, Charcoal Black text, 1px solid `#151515` border. On hover, shifts to Warm Off-White (`#F4F1E8`) with a hard offset: `box-shadow: 2px 2px 0px #151515`.
- **Tertiary / Utility**: Plain text with a permanent bottom 1px Charcoal underline, snapping to Cobalt Blue upon pointer focus.

### Chips & Badges
- Strictly rectangular with a 6px radius.
- **Prospectus Verified (RAG)**: Cobalt Blue background (`#1647D8`), pure white text, prefaced with a minimal 4px white square marker.
- **Practical Exam Type (기초디자인 / 조형)**: Light Warm Off-White background, 1px Charcoal border, Charcoal text.
- **Critical Risk (Cut-off Fall)**: Vermilion Red background (`#E34234`), pure white text, accompanied by an equilateral triangle indicator.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text & Numeric Inputs**: Surface White container, 1px solid `#151515` stroke, 8px radius, `Space Grotesk` tabular numerals.
- **Focused State**: Border width expands to 2px in Cobalt Blue (`#1647D8`) with no halo or diffused glow.
- **Checkboxes**: Pure squares with a 1px solid Charcoal border. Checked state renders a solid inner Charcoal square block rather than a soft organic checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons**: Pure circles with a 1px solid Charcoal border. Selected state renders a centered concentric solid circle.

### Cards & Layout Modules
- **University Matrix Card**: Surface White fill, 1px solid `#151515` border, 8px border radius. Displays a top structural metadata banner containing the school code, recruitment season (수시/정시), and quota, delimited by a hairline horizontal divider.
- **RAG Evidence Inspector**: Off-White tint surface inset into White cards, featuring a solid 2px Cobalt Blue left border, rendering verbatim excerpts from official university recruitment prospectuses accompanied by exact PDF source page pagination.
- **Practical Exam Timeline Card**: Horizontal grid strip mapping out setup time, ideation phase, and drawing time in high-contrast blocked intervals.
